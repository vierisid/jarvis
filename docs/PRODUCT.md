# Brief product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Founders using Jarvis to turn repetitive work into reliable programmatic workflows, with AI where judgment is needed. Goals, awareness, memory and authority make that work relevant and controlled. The dashboard should make the next decision and the outcome of completed work immediately understandable.

## Approved direction

Calm, precise, useful. The native **Brief · Pebble workspace v6.1** page in Figma (`eNfKeirIKLZLlZEasrurZa`, page `81:2`) is the visual source of truth. Checkpoint: **UI-35 · Dashboard craft pass verified · prototype limits recorded**. Per-task extractions and evidence live in `docs/brief-delivery/` and the corresponding `ui/src/brief/` module. Those sources, rather than an independently inferred theme, govern implementation.

## Design principles

- Preserve orientation and the identity of the work through layout changes.
- Make the outcome and next action clearer than the machinery behind them.
- Give each control one precise purpose and consistent feedback.
- Use motion to explain where an object came from and where it went.
- Keep the existing brand, typography and asymmetric corner families.

## Anti-references

No glass conversation overlay, decorative page-load choreography, miniature text to rescue crowded layouts, or repeated explanatory subtitles. Do not revive removed global creation controls, workspace selector or chat branding. Do not turn a shell task into a redesign of approved rooms.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Keyboard alternatives, visible focus, forgiving hit areas, readable narrow layouts and OS reduced motion are implementation requirements. Color must not be the only indicator of state. Browser evidence is not a claim of screen-reader or WCAG certification.
