## Result and scope

The released sidebar groups rooms into clusters and uses a 64px collapsed state. This change supplies the approved destination-based Brief navigation: a 232px sidebar, a 72px rail, a clean breadcrumb header and the Profile / Settings / Billing account menu. It uses the existing official logo, colors, controls and D-04 motion.

The mark stays at the same optical anchor while the wordmark fades. The collapse target stays at x16/y60. Header and content share a 38px gutter; content keeps its left alignment as the working area recovers 160px. Navigation rows keep their vertical positions and reserve a fixed count column. Healthy connection status stays still in a 40px inspectable target. A neutral state explicitly says when connection status is unavailable.

The entire name/avatar/plan block opens the account menu. The menu opens above that block or beside the rail, inherits appearance, supports arrows/Home/End/typeahead, and dismisses on outside interaction, Escape or Tab. Escape and selection restore the account origin; outside clicks retain the user's new focus. A reopened menu can reverse its exit using the same motion object. Rail tooltips have a continuous pointer bridge and keyboard descriptions.

This is the reusable shell and its D-01 integration, not a new implementation of the room bodies. The review controls in the screenshots are explicitly marked fixtures. Profile/Settings/Billing destinations navigate correctly; their real forms belong to their later room tasks. D-06 owns Pebble placement/reflow. The fixture's conversation switch verifies retained state only.
