## Result

Today has its approved greeting/date and decision-first composition. The main goal retains its position near the top when Pebble opens, moving into a compact slot above the conversation. Its component stays mounted. Outcomes precede Recent activity, and Opportunities keeps its heading outside the card.

The hero paper rests inside the diagonal recess. Pointer intent raises and slightly straightens that paper, with matching rounded backing/shadow layers. The summary does not rewrite or move on hover. At most two backing sheets are shown. Approval, Keep draft and rejection controls are present only when the supplied decision permits and names them. They can be reached directly on the raised paper, without a click to pin it.

The always-visible Review button opens the full document inside Today and brings it into view. Close/Escape restores the initiating button and the stored main scroll. Layout-induced pointer entry after closing cannot raise the paper again; a new pointer movement or keyboard-visible focus can. A mouse-clicked action does not keep the tray raised just because its button retained focus.

Loading, no decisions, stale reads, unavailable reads and unresolved effects are distinct. Stale or pending decisions cannot be approved. An approved action is not displayed as executed unless its supplied execution outcome is committed. D-07 neither advances the queue nor performs a domain write.

## Integration for Lapo and other implementers

1. Use a separate Git worktree per task. Stack real prerequisites without merging them automatically. Keep F/Q worktrees and domain contracts untouched.
2. `createTodayRoom(useToday)` is an optional room-local registration factory. Create it once in the composition root and supply the existing subscribed owner through `useToday`. Do not construct it during every render or start another poller/transport in Today.
3. The owner supplies greeting/date, a `DecisionBinding`, and independently gated `TodaySlots`. There are no fabricated live outcome counts or goal fallbacks. Use the supplied account/timezone convention for greeting/date.
4. `DecisionPaper` carries the exact F-01 `BriefDecision`, its revision, document text, summary, allowed-action labels and nullable queue count. Use F-12's verified presentation. Do not parse raw tool arguments to invent recipients or documents. Never label an action “Approve & send” unless that is its actual supplied meaning.
5. Live decisions require `source: "live"` and the existing enabled/ready `decisions` capability. Fixture/live mismatches fail closed. The default registration has no Body, so this PR does not enable an unfinished live room.
6. `onAction(decision, action)` hands the exact identity/revision to the existing operation owner. That owner must synchronously publish matching pending state, catch/report operation failures and refresh authoritative data. The paper suppresses same-dispatch duplicate clicks and respects supplied pending state; it is not an idempotency or concurrency controller. D-08 completes that presentation flow, with F-12/F-14 enforcing writes.
7. Keep the current paper mounted across shell changes. A new decision ID/revision resets transient review state. Queue-count changes alone retain the same front paper. Never key a room by theme, sidebar or chat state.
8. `PebbleCompanion` is a single room-owned presentation slot above chat. Supply one goal element from Today. Its stable portal preserves component state as width changes; do not mount duplicate expanded/compact goal owners. The target's `data-compact` expresses the layout. Other rooms retain D-06's existing panel geometry when the slot is empty.
9. Preserve the `brief-work` container, main scroll owner and D-04 motion controller. The hero also has a `brief-decision` container: below 580px the summary/paper stack vertically. Full review text remains available rather than being squeezed into the decorative excerpt.
