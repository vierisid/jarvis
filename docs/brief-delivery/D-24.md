## State and numerical rules

`GoalsController` belongs to one authenticated scope and survives room mounts. `GoalsPort.read` provides a complete authorized root collection with explicitly ordered paths. The room never treats a first page as the collection or calculates a parent total by adding children. Read generations suppress late responses. Invalid or failed refreshes preserve qualified stale content, disable next-step navigation and expose the read reason. Revocation clears cached data and positions; retire the controller on logout/scope replacement.

Selection is keyed by stable goal ID, separately for each view/filter. Reordering does not choose another goal. Returning from Completed restores the active selection. Selectors scroll locally when numerous and preserve their position. Active/Completed remain the two main views; a quiet Status filter exposes Paused, Failed, Draft and Killed without renaming them or adding an Archive destination.

F-15's normalized `progress.value` determines the shared segment treatment. Displayed numerator, target, unit and baseline come only from a qualified measurement. A legacy score is explicitly **Score**, never invented as six signed partners out of ten. Unknown progress is distinct from an observed zero. Nonzero baselines and decreasing targets retain their raw values and units; normalized progress is not recalculated from raw value/target. Progress percentages are displayed to one decimal place without rounding the underlying fraction. Completed status does not force a count to its target. The positive weekly change is separate evidence and does not alter goal progress.

Data basis discloses qualification, baseline, timestamp and source references. Illustrative measurements are labeled User reported. Numbers and segments reuse Today's components and color rule. Only changed glyphs/segments update; navigation does not count up from zero and completed milestones do not replay a celebration.
